Return Policy

General Returns

At Denncathy Fresh Basket, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not satisfied with your order, we offer returns within the following conditions:

Return Window

Fresh produce and perishable items can be returned within 24 hours of delivery if they arrive damaged, spoiled, or not as described.

Conditions for Returns

  • Products must be in original, unopened packaging (where applicable)
  • Products must not show signs of consumption or misuse
  • Return must be initiated within the specified timeframe
  • Proof of purchase (order number and receipt) must be provided

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please:

  1. Contact us immediately with photos of the damage
  2. Provide your order number and date of delivery
  3. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no additional cost

Refund Process

Once your return is approved:

  • You will receive a prepaid return label (for non-food items)
  • Processing time: 5-7 business days after item receipt
  • Refunds will be issued to your original payment method
  • Shipping costs may not be refundable for non-defective items

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Perishable items that have passed their expiry date
  • Items that show signs of consumption
  • Items purchased during clearance sales (final sale)

Contact Us

For questions about returns or to initiate a return, please:

  • Email: support@denncathy.co.ke
  • Phone: +254 712 345 678
  • Visit our Contact page

Policy Changes

We reserve the right to modify this return policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
